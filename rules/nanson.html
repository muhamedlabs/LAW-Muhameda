We are here for music, new releases and good company. To keep it comfortable for everyone, we put together a few simple rules — they apply across the whole Nanson community.

These rules supplement our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. In case of conflict, the main documents take priority.

01

Respect each other

Music brings people together, not apart.

  1. 1.1Not allowed

    No insults or harassment

    Don’t insult, harass or demean other members.

  2. 1.2Not allowed

    No discrimination or threats

    Discrimination, threats and provocations are not allowed.

  3. 1.3Allowed

    Tastes differ

    You can and should debate music — just don’t mock other people’s taste.

  4. 1.4Important

    Be kind to beginners

    Don’t make fun of people who are still learning to play, sing or mix — give them a hand instead.

  5. 1.5Important

    Critique the track, not the person

    Explain what didn’t work and how it could be improved.

02

Copyright-free music

Free music for everyone — played fair.

  1. 2.1Important

    Free-to-use music only

    Share tracks that can genuinely be used freely, and state the terms if there are any.

  2. 2.2Not allowed

    No piracy

    Don’t post illegal copies of music, paid samples or plugins.

  3. 2.3Not allowed

    Don’t pass off others’ work

    Don’t present someone else’s tracks, beats or remixes as your own.

  4. 2.4Not allowed

    Don’t claim what isn’t yours

    Don’t register other people’s tracks with copyright-protection systems or file claims against their use.

  5. 2.5Important

    Credit the artists

    Share other people’s music with a link to the artist and the source.

  6. 2.6Important

    Remixes and samples

    If you use someone else’s sample or make a remix, say so and respect the licence terms.

03

Privacy and safety

Your data and your peace of mind stay protected.

  1. 3.1Not allowed

    Other people’s personal data

    Don’t post other people’s personal data without their consent.

  2. 3.2Not allowed

    Unacceptable content

    18+ material, shock content and scenes of cruelty are not allowed.

  3. 3.3Important

    Nicknames and avatars

    The same rules apply to nicknames and avatars.

  4. 3.4Not allowed

    Don’t pry

    Don’t ask other members for passwords, codes from messages or other personal data.

  5. 3.5Not allowed

    Suspicious links and files

    Don’t send suspicious links or files. If you see one, tell the staff.

04

One person, one account

You are you, and you have one account.

  1. 4.1Not allowed

    Just one account

    Multiple accounts are not allowed.

  2. 4.2Not allowed

    No bots or scripts

    Bots, scripts and macros are not allowed.

  3. 4.3Important

    A real person writes

    Every message, comment and rating must come from a real person.

  4. 4.4Important

    Your account is yours alone

    Don’t give anyone access to your account and don’t use someone else’s.

  5. 4.5Not allowed

    Fake numbers are cheating

    Don’t inflate plays, likes, followers or votes.

05

No spam or ads

Chat is for music, not for noise.

  1. 5.1Not allowed

    No flooding

    Flooding, repeats, all caps and mass mentions are not allowed.

  2. 5.2Not allowed

    No advertising

    Advertising without the staff’s approval is not allowed.

  3. 5.3Important

    Your own music, in the right place

    Share your tracks where the channel’s topic fits, and don’t send them in private messages unasked.

  4. 5.4Important

    One topic per channel

    Stay on topic in each channel and don’t duplicate a message across channels.

  5. 5.5Allowed

    Share your discoveries

    Found a great track, album or artist that fits the channel? Share it — that’s what this community is for.

06

Listen to the staff

Moderators are here so everyone feels comfortable.

  1. 6.1Not allowed

    No debates in the main chat

    Moderator decisions aren’t discussed in the main chat.

  2. 6.2Not allowed

    Punishments can’t be bypassed

    Punishments can’t be bypassed with other accounts.

  3. 6.3Allowed

    Disagree? Message the staff

    Disagree with a decision? Calmly message the staff.

  4. 6.4Important

    Spotted a violation?

    Don’t answer in kind — tell the staff and we’ll sort it out.

  5. 6.5Important

    Rules may be updated

    If we change the rules, the current date is shown at the top of this page.
